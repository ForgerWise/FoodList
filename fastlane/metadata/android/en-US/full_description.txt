FoodList helps you use food before it goes bad — simply.

• Add in two taps: pick a common food and its name, category and typical shelf life are filled in. Your recent items come first.
• Scan barcodes: EAN / JAN / UPC product codes fill in the name; GS1 QR and DataMatrix codes can even fill in the expiry date. Names you type are remembered.
• See what matters: red = expired, orange = expiring soon, green = fresh. Tap a counter to filter.
• "Used one" and Undo: swipe to use up or delete — mistakes are one tap away from fixed.
• Daily reminder at the time you choose.
• Your own categories and emoji icons.
• Light and dark mode. English, 繁體中文, 日本語.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your list stays on your phone. Barcode lookups send only the barcode number.

Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/ForgerWise/FoodList

Product data: Open Food Facts (ODbL).
